We are excited to begin enrollment in our longitudinal cohort focused on autoimmune arthritis across pediatric and adult populations.

The study aligns immunophenotyping, clinical metadata, and antigen discovery assays to identify shared and age-modulated immune signatures.

This effort was developed in close partnership with clinical collaborators and patient advisory representatives.
